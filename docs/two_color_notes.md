# Two-color excitation: notes

- Lim & Saloma (2002) rotate beam 2 in real space, not in optical coordinates
  as their equation on p. 122 is written. The optical-coordinate rotation gives
  −0.21 x_c² for the θ = π/2 in-plane peak loss instead of their −0.06 x_c²;
  the real-space rotation gives −0.063 x_c².
