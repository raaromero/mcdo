Numerical aperture

NA 0.5 in air is the setting of the paper.

Medium

Signal

Direction

Angle between the lenses

θ = π/2 stops at a lens half-angle of 45° (NA 0.71 in air, 0.94 in water), where the two crossed cones would meet.

Model

Solid: vector (Richards–Wolf). Dashed: paraxial scalar, the model of the paper.

Annular: a ring with obstruction ratio ε = 0.99, as in Figs. 6–7.

Input beam

Gaussian: truncated at the pupil edge, α = 4.
